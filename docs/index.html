FAQ

Why is this week empty? In the offseason there are no games to show. Use the season picker (top right of the Scores tab) to browse the completed season, back to 2014.

Why does a game appear in multiple sections? On purpose. Following, Top 25, and conference sections are each complete — a ranked team you follow shows up in all three.

Where do scores come from? Publicly available sports data services, refreshed automatically while games are live.
