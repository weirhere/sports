## X / Twitter thread

1. Been building this alone since July: a football scores app with no ads,
   no accounts, no tracking. College football and now the NFL, one screen,
   in black and white.

2. Follow your teams, either league, and they lead the page. Everything
   else is the Top 25 and every conference, as accordions you can collapse.

3. Saturday and Sunday aren't the same calendar, so the app is a day at a
   time now, not a week. Swipe to the next day, jump back to Today whenever
   you've wandered off.

4. Live games get a pulsing dot and a possession marker, and the score
   itself gets heavier type. Nothing else on the screen is trying to
   compete with it.

5. New this week: the postseason draws as an actual bracket, on the NFL's
   page and the Top 25 both. Who plays whom, round by round, byes shown
   where a team sat one out. Swipe between rounds.

6. What it doesn't do: no ads, no interstitials, no account, no tracking.
   It talks straight to ESPN's API and keeps your followed teams on your
   phone and nowhere else.

7. Free. Two leagues, done right. [App Store link]
