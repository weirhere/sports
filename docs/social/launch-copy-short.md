# Launch copy, short form

Second pass on `launch-copy-video.md`. The feedback on those drafts was
"rewrite it in human words", and Andy pointed at 2 posts as the target:

- **Cole (@colderoshay, X, 2026-10-16):** "i built a little chrome extension
  that surfaces replies when you're on someone's profile. Drop a ✋ if you want
  to try it!" A 10s video and nothing else. 1,900 views, 21 likes, 7 bookmarks,
  5 replies on a small account.
- **Carlos (@carlosmlon, Threads, 2026-10-18):** "Adding micro-animations to
  an app can bring so much joy. Personally, I find them delightful without
  being distracting. What do you think?" 3 screenshots and an App Store link.
  9K views, 138 likes, 18 comments.

What they share: 1 thing, said once, in the first person, then a question.
No feature list, no tagline, no price. Neither names the product in the post
body (Carlos puts it under the fold, Cole never does). The video or the
screenshots are the product; the words are the person.

What the first drafts did instead, measured against that:

- Every post carried the same pitch ("your teams at the top, refreshed every
  second, any game 1 tap from the full story"). Read 8 times in a row, it's
  an ad.
- "Free on iPhone" closed 6 of the 8 posts. Once, in the first reply, is enough.
- Posts opened by explaining the product rather than saying what happened.

## Rules for this pass

1. 1 sentence on what I made or noticed. 1 on why, or how it feels. 1 ask.
   That's the whole post.
2. Lowercase opener is fine on X and Threads (Cole does it; it reads as typed,
   not published). Sentence case everywhere else.
3. Name and link go in the first reply, never the post.
4. The video and screenshots carry the feature list. A caption never repeats
   what the picture already shows.

Each platform gets 2 options: an **"i built a thing"** post (Cole's shape) and
a **"here's something I noticed"** post (Carlos's shape). Pick 1 per platform,
and don't run both the same week.

## Posting notes

- Post the video with no link in the body. Link in the first reply, a minute
  later. X and LinkedIn both push down posts with outbound links.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour, and ask something back ("which
  conference?"). The 1-word answers are the engagement, and they double as a
  list of team pages to spot-check.
- "No 30 tabs" is Andy's ESPN line from the r/CFB draft, without naming ESPN.
  Fine on his own account.
- Timing from the first file holds: Saturday morning before the early games,
  or Sunday before 1pm ET.
- League names stay out of App Store metadata (the 4.1(a) rejection). These
  are organic posts from a person, a different context, but the 2 Threads and
  X options above happen to name none anyway.
