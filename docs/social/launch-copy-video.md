# Launch copy: the 14s video spot

Posts to run with `StatSide-14s-vertical-1080x1920.mp4` and
`StatSide-14s-horizontal-1920x1080.mp4`. Organic only. Same voice as
`launch-copy-2.1.md`: one person who built the thing, talking plainly.

Every post ends on a question a fan can answer in one word (usually a team
name). Those comments are what the algorithms reward, and they double as a
list of which teams to spot-check in the app.

Links: Instagram and TikTok don't link from captions, so StatSide.co goes in
the bio. X and LinkedIn push down posts with outbound links, so the link goes
in the first reply or comment.

## LinkedIn (horizontal)

**Post**
> I've spent the last few months building StatSide, an iPhone scores app, on nights and weekends.
>
> The brief I gave myself: open the app, see your teams, done. A few decisions that shaped it:
>
> • Black and white everywhere. Color only shows up for team logos and live games, so your eye goes where the action is.
> • Live games refresh every second. Speed is the product.
> • No account, no ads, no analytics. Your followed teams stay on your phone.
> • The day is the unit of time. Football, basketball and hockey all keep different calendars, so a "week" meant something different to each one.
>
> Most of the work was deciding what to leave out.

## Posting notes

- **When:** Saturday morning before the early kickoffs, or Sunday before
  1pm ET. People are already in score-checking mode then.
- **First hour:** reply to every comment. A reply that asks something back
  ("Nice, which conference?") doubles the thread.
- **League names:** the store listing keeps league marks out of its fields
  (the 4.1(a) rejection). Hashtags in organic posts are a different context,
  but if that ever becomes a worry, swap #nba and #nhl for #basketball and
  #hockey.
