## Decision

**The NBA Cup's first edition counts, and every probed pro postseason headline is pinned verbatim.** "NBA In-Season Tournament Championship", the 2023 final the Lakers won, now reads as the NBA Cup, while its group play and earlier rounds still win nothing. `ProPostseasonHeadlines` on iOS and its twin in `trophies.test.ts` hold the exact strings a live probe of all five pro leagues returned for 2014–25, both the titles and the earlier rounds that must stay silent.

## Why

A second, independent probe the same day as #314 confirmed its findings (the " - Game N" tail, the conference round's respellings) and found one more silent miss: the cup's 2023 edition predates the "NBA Cup" name, so the matcher never saw it. It is the same trophy, which the NBA counts as the first NBA Cup. Pinning the probed strings verbatim means the next ESPN respelling breaks a test rather than emptying a shelf without anyone noticing.
