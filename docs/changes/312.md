## Decision

**Athlete search drops college basketball players whose school has no page, the way it already did for football.** `AthleteSearchStore.inScope` filters every college league against that league's directory names: FBS and FCS for football, Division I for both basketball leagues. Search's "mcdav" stops offering Tim McDavid of Chadron State, a Division II school. A college league whose directory is empty lets its players through, because a failed standings request says nothing about who plays in it. The pro leagues stay unfiltered.

**One row per person, not per ESPN id.** ESPN keeps some players under two ids: "mcdav" sent Wofford's Jamari McDavid as `4281043`, with a headshot, and again as "Jamari Mcdavid", `4902079`, without one. Results are now deduplicated on league, case-folded name and team, and the first, higher-ranked entry is kept.

## Why

A row that opens nothing is worse than no row. That rule filtered college football on 2026-09-21, before college basketball joined the app, and nobody extended it when the basketball leagues arrived (2026-11-02). The men's and women's directories were checked live: 365 and 363 schools, with Wofford and Arkansas State in them and Chadron State in neither. Their `displayName`s match search's `subtitle` exactly. Deduplicating on name over id could merge two people with one name on one roster, which is rarer than the duplicate it removes. The empty-directory pass-through keeps a failed standings request from hiding a whole league.

## Web parity

shipped — the web already drops Division II players, since `linkableAthletes` resolves every hit's club against the directory to build its link. It now deduplicates on league, name and club too, with two new tests in `athlete-search.test.ts`.
