## Decision

**College football's national titles go all-time, counted by the AP poll through 1997 and the title game since.** `TrophyRegistry` gets its first verified list, `collegeFootballNationalTitles` (in `TrophyRegistry+CollegeFootball.swift`): 77 seasons, with the AP champion for 1936–1997, the BCS game's winner for 1998–2013 and no row for USC's vacated 2004. The CFP era stays derived from the wire. A list can now carry a `source`, which `TrophyCase.assemble` passes onto the all-time row as `TrophyGroup.source`. The Trophies card prints it on its own caption line, "National Championships: AP poll 1936–97 · title game since 1998", above the existing "… since 2014" line. Alabama's shelf reads 12: nine from the registry, three CFP games derived.

## Why

This applies COR-94's first call (Andy, 2026-11-04). Andy's note there says the caption has to name the source, because the count depends on which source was picked: Alabama reads 18 by its own claims and 12 by this rule. The rows were sourced from Wikipedia's FBS national championships table and checked against the AP National Championship Trophy page, whose year-by-year list and per-school totals both agree on every season. Every ESPN id is resolved in the tests from a school name in the captured FBS standings, so a mistyped id fails as a wrong total, not as someone else's title. The per-program totals (27 programs, summing to 77) come from the AP table, adjusted for the only two seasons where the BCS game and the AP disagree, 2003 and 2004. A dropped, doubled or misfiled season moves at least one of them.

## Web parity

shipped — `web/src/lib/trophy-registry-college-football.ts` is generated row for row from the Swift list. `registrySources` and `TrophyGroup.source` mirror iOS, and the card's footnote prints the same two lines. `trophy-registry.test.ts` checks the same span, totals and caption.
