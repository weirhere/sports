## Decision

**Tapping a venue opens it in Apple Maps.** The shared `VenueHeadline`, which
leads both the game page's and the team page's Venue card, becomes a button
to an Apple Maps search for "name, city" (`MapsLink`), with a trailing
`arrow.up.forward` as the leaves-the-app mark.

## Why

Andy asked for it in #feedback on 2026-11-03 (COR-151). Apple Maps directly
rather than a chooser, because iOS has no default-maps setting outside the
EU and a chooser needs `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes` for apps nobody asked
for. It's a search because ESPN sends no coordinates. An arrow rather than a
chevron, because a chevron in this app means a pushed page.

## Web parity

shipped — `web/src/components/venue-headline.tsx` links to the identical maps.apple.com URL in a new tab.
