## Decision

**The Starters card matches the Figma sheet "↳ Roster variants"** (file `7aRWypk5xbB2GnpRlcKkQV`, page `2375:2`, section 1).

- **The surface.** Every surface is 736pt at the default text size, 368 a half, with no empty band at either end. Its rows sit between its markings.
  - Football: eight 92pt bands with no yard lines or hashes; the line of scrimmage is 2pt of chalk.
  - Court: a 140pt frontcourt and 228pt backcourt. The key (30% of the width, 22% of the length), its free-throw circle, the centre line and the centre circle (as wide as the key) are 4pt lines in the paint.
  - Rink: a 110pt goalie row (the goalie stands flush toward his defense), a 120pt pair and 138pt forwards. The red line, the goal lines (20pt off the end), the half-circle creases opening into the ice and the centre circle are 4pt red. The blue lines fall between the pair and the forwards at 20%, so the red carries the rink. The boards outline is gone.
- **The players.** The disc is 48pt (it was 32) with its name 4pt under it. It keeps its light-mode grays in dark mode.
- **Football strips.** A strip at each end, 8pt above and below its chips, holds the team's 28pt logo, bare with `LogoImage`'s sticker outline against the turf, and Offense / Defense chips. The two strips always read opposite each other and drive the existing `offenseTeamId`. They replace the "HOU offense" chip row and both field captions.
- **Court and rink bars.** These are 48pt bars with a 32pt logo and the team's name, one at each end: away between the title and the surface, home under it. The name is spelled out ("Miami Heat") in 14pt medium under the title's 13pt semibold (Andy, 2026-10-28). `iceHeading` is a neutral #dcdcdc.
- **Chips.** `SlateToggleChip` on a painted surface is 32pt in the layout, with its tap target reaching past it. Its idle outline is at full ink strength. The Games tab's chips keep their 44pt row.
- **Dark mode turns the Starters surfaces gray.** The field is `bgElevated` with `bgHeader` bands. The heading, paint and every line are `divider`, including the rink's red and blue lines. Court and rink text and chips are adaptive. This is `SurfaceColors.Starters`; the live Gamecast surfaces keep their colors.

## Why

Andy and Matt redesigned the card on the sheet (2026-10-27). Andy's instruction was *"Implement the updated figma designs into the codebase"*, given after he was told the sheet's dark mode changes the color budget.

That **amends the color budget's fourth exception, for the Starters card only: in dark mode its surfaces are monochrome.** A gray field doesn't picture turf, maple or ice. It reads as a darker card with markings rather than a lit pitch on a black page. Light mode is unchanged and still pictures the place. Andy approved implementing the Figma designs. Asked two follow-up questions, he said he had no preference on either, so both follow the sheet and the session's recommendation, and both are easy to revisit:

- **The rink's red goes gray in dark mode, with the rest of its lines.** The sheet's Theme/Dark mode maps `rinkRed` to `surface/boards`, and the sheet is the source of truth. Keeping the red is one line in `SurfaceColors.Starters`.
- **Scope is the Starters card alone, with its own dark values (`SurfaceColors.Starters`).** `DriveField`, `CourtSurface` and `RinkSurface` share the light paint but weren't on the sheet, so they stay colored in dark mode. Extending the grays to them means pointing them at `SurfaceColors.Starters`.

The fixed heights replace a row height that scaled with the slot. That left empty bands at the field's ends, and it put avatars across lines.
