## Decision

**The Starters card matches the Figma sheet "↳ Roster variants"** (file `7aRWypk5xbB2GnpRlcKkQV`, page `2375:2`, section 1).

- **The surface.** Every surface is 736pt at the default text size, 368 a half, with no empty band at either end. Its rows sit between its markings: football's eight 92pt bands with no yard lines, hashes or scrimmage line; the court's 140pt frontcourt and 228pt backcourt; the rink's 110pt goalie (flush toward his defense), 120pt pair and 138pt forwards. Court and rink lines are 4pt. The court is outlined in paint: the key (30% of the width, 22% of the length), its free-throw circle, the centre line and circle. On the rink, the red line, goal lines (20pt off the end), creases and centre circle are all red, and the blue lines fall between the pair and the forwards. The rink loses its boards outline.
- **The players.** The disc is 48pt (it was 32) with its name 4pt under it. It keeps its light-mode grays in dark mode.
- **Football strips.** A strip at each end, 8pt above and below its chips, holds the team's 28pt logo, bare with `LogoImage`'s sticker outline against the turf, and Offense / Defense chips. The two strips always read opposite each other and drive the existing `offenseTeamId`. They replace the "HOU offense" chip row and both field captions.
- **Court and rink bars.** These are 48pt bars with a 32pt logo and the team's name. The court has one, under the floor, for the home side. The rink has one at each end.
- **Chips.** `SlateToggleChip` on a painted surface is 32pt in the layout, with its tap target reaching past it. Its idle outline is at full ink strength. The Games tab's chips keep their 44pt row.
- **Dark mode turns the Starters surfaces gray.** The field is `bgElevated` with `bgHeader` bands. The heading, paint and every line are `divider`, including the rink's red and blue lines. Court and rink text and chips are adaptive. This is `SurfaceColors.Starters`; the live Gamecast surfaces keep their colors.

## Why

Andy and Matt redesigned the card on the sheet (2026-10-27). Andy's instruction was *"Implement the updated figma designs into the codebase"*, given after he was told the sheet's dark mode changes the color budget.

That **amends the color budget's fourth exception** for the Starters card in dark mode. A gray field doesn't picture turf, maple or ice. It reads as a darker card with markings, rather than a lit pitch on a black page. Light mode is unchanged and still pictures the place.

The sheet's Theme/Dark mode maps `rinkRed` to `surface/boards`, so the rink's one red goes gray in dark with the rest of the lines. Reverting that is one line in `SurfaceColors.Starters`. The live Gamecast surfaces share the light paint but weren't on the sheet, so their dark mode is untouched.

The fixed heights replace a row height that scaled with the slot. That left empty bands at the field's ends, and it put avatars across lines.
