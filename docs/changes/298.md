## Decision

**The Leagues page's top fade waits for a scroll, with the search field's
shadow.** The 20pt fade over the top of the list (`ScrollEdgeFade`) is hidden
while the list is at rest and eases in on the same `isScrolled` flag that has
held back the field's shadow since 2026-10-26, so the two arrive and leave
together.

## Why

Andy, from a screenshot with nothing followed: the field "has a shadow that's
casting on the NBA card", which should only appear once the input is sitting
on top of the cards. The field's shadow was already off at rest; the gray was
the fade painting over the first card's top edge. With a league followed the
Following heading sits in that band on the page's ground, which is why it
never showed before. At rest nothing has run under the chrome, so there is
nothing for the fade to fade out.

## Web parity

shipped — the hub's sticky head holds its `after:` fade and the field's shadow (`SearchField`'s new `showsShadow`) until the window scrolls past the top.
