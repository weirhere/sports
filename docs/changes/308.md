## Decision

**A completed week keeps the ranks its games were played at, with no snapshot of our own.** When Sunday's poll lands, ESPN doesn't re-rank the finished week's events: Week 9's finals, probed five days after the Nov 1 AP Week 10 poll, still carry the Week 9 poll on every ranked team, dropped teams included. The rows and the Top 25 section of a Sunday-pinned week stay as they were on Saturday, and the Polls tab being newer than the slate is correct. Nothing is built.

## Why

The backlog item had been parked since 2026-09-01 on exactly this observation. If ESPN flipped `curatedRank`, the finals would show ranks the teams didn't carry into the game and `Game.isRanked` would move games in and out of the Top 25 under the user, and each row would need a rank captured at final. It doesn't flip. Every ranked Week 9 team's rank equals its `previous` in the new poll: Ohio State #2 (now #11), Indiana #4 (now #6), and SMU, Northwestern, Oklahoma State and Wisconsin still ranked after dropping out. The same probe found that upcoming events carry the CFP committee's rank once it exists (Alabama #5, Kentucky #6, against #9 and #8 in AP). That's recorded here, not acted on.
