## Decision

**The News tab has four pages: For you · Latest · Transfers · Leagues.** The
four league tabs (NCAAF, NFL, NBA, NHL) fold into one Leagues page that lists
every league in the Leagues tab's order (`League.displayOrder`: NBA, NCAAF,
NFL, NHL), each as a heading with "See more" and its five newest stories as
full-width stacked cards — photo on top, headline under it — never the
thumbnail row. Latest, the full-width list that closed For you, is its own
page and leaves For you, which now ends at its last team section.
`NewsFeedStore` fetches the four league feeds once and cuts Latest, Transfers,
Trending and the Leagues page from that one list.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19: "combine the cards on each News tab for each League into a
single League tab… listed in the order that they have them arranged in the
leagues nav item"; the cards "vertically stacked (image on top, text on
bottom), not a horizontal card". Five tabs were four of the same page with a
different filter; one page shows the whole slate in a scroll, and the stacked
card is the one the photos were brought in for. Latest moved rather than
duplicated so For you is the personal page and nothing else (Andy's pick
between the two in planning). Supersedes `docs/decisions.md` row 369's "a
league page is one section" and the five-tab row of row 358.

## Decision

**A Transfers page, by reading the headline.** `NewsStory.isTransaction` marks
a story as a roster move when its headline or dek carries a whole-word trade,
signing, waiver, release, extension, free-agency, portal or commitment term,
or when an ESPN topic names one outright (`trade deadline`, `transfer portal`,
`transactions`). Recaps and previews never qualify. The page is the merged
all-leagues list filtered by it, uncapped.

## Why

Andy asked for "a filtered list of just transfer related news articles."
Probed 2026-10-19: ESPN's `topic` categories cover almost none of them —
across 50-story NFL, NBA and NHL feeds the only transaction tag was one
`nba free agency`, which sat on a stat column, while "Knicks waive Bruce
Brown" and "Still awaiting trade, Connor Hellebuyck…" carried no topic at all.
A keyword reading is the only signal there is, so it is written as a pure,
tested function with its own list of words, and no bare "deal", "cut" or
"sign": every game story has a deal in it and a lead gets cut every night.
`docs/news.md` N16's college half stays parked; this is prose, not FotMob's
from–to cards.

## Web parity

shipped — `isTransaction` and `TRANSACTION_TERMS` in `web/src/lib/news.ts`, the term list copied verbatim from iOS; the mapper keeps `topic` categories as `topics`.

## Decision

**For you gets a team filter sheet.** A filter button on the News masthead
(For you only, in the slot Teams gives its plus) opens a medium/large sheet:
a checklist of every followed team in the Teams tab's A–Z order, a footer
saying unchecked teams keep their follow and leave this page, and a "Manage
teams" row that closes the sheet and switches to the Teams tab. Unchecked
teams drop out of For you's sections and out of its per-team fetch. Stored as
`news.hiddenTeams` on `UIStateStore`. Done and a swipe both dismiss.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19: "a settings affordance that opens a sheet to control what
teams show up on this page. Just checkboxes is fine… Sorting is based on the
order of your favorites. Link to your favorites." Follows have no order of
their own (a set, shown A–Z on the Teams tab), so the sheet keeps that order
and E26's "Follow order" P3 stays open. Supersedes `docs/news.md` N12's "no
news filter sheet"; N12's other half holds — it filters teams, not interests.
The link goes to the Teams tab rather than the Add-teams sheet because that is
where follows are managed, and the router already has the hook the Scores
follow prompt uses.

## Decision

**Live and Tight are one menu.** The Scores header's capsule holds a `SlateFocusChip` — a menu reading "All games", "Live" or "Tight" with the season chip's chevron, the chosen row marked with a check at the end of its name and Tight alone carrying a line under it ("Live and close, late in the game, or an upset brewing") — and the calendar, where the Live and Tight pills were. `SlateFocus` is the three-way reading of the two stored filters, which stay as they were (`liveOnly`, `tightOnly`, exclusive, today-only); choosing Live or Tight off today is still the trip home, and All games clears whichever is on. Live wears the live tint, edge and dot; Tight the ink wash; All games nothing.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19: "Live and Tight are taking up more real estate than I would prefer. I wonder if we combine these into a dropdown." The two were already exclusive (2026-09-24), so they were one choice drawn as two buttons; one chip gives the row back half the capsule's width. The fills and the trip-home rule carry over unchanged so nothing the pills promised is lost, and the storage is untouched so a saved filter survives the update. Tight's description is there because it is a coined word (Andy asked whether the rows should have them); All games and Live had lines for an hour and lost them the same day, since they say what they are. The check is a trailing glyph because iOS 26 menus draw a row's image at the leading edge, and a leading check on one row indented it alone.

## Decision

**Pinned search chrome sits on the page's own ground, and the list fades under it.** The Leagues tab's masthead and field, and the Search tab's field at the foot, paint `bgRecessed` like the page instead of a lighter bar; a 20pt `ScrollEdgeFade` over the scroll's edge thins the content to nothing as it runs under them, and `SearchField`'s shadow grows (0.12, 12pt, y 3) since it is now what lifts the field. Page titles drop one weight, heavy to bold. Web: the Leagues masthead and field pin under the nav bar on `bg-recessed` with a gradient after-element; the Search footer bar does the same above itself and loses its rule; the field is card-colored with a `shadow-field` token (none in dark); `type-page-heading` is 700.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19: the Leagues header "should have the same background as the rest of the page but as the user scrolls the content fades out behind instead, the search input also needs a shadow to elevate it off the surface"; the Search input "should follow the same pattern"; and "drop the weight of the page titles by one weight." A lighter bar drew a line across the page the content stopped at; one ground with a fade keeps the page one surface and the field is lifted by shadow, which is the job a shadow has. Bold rather than heavy at 28 because the name is now the biggest type on the page and heavy at that size read as a wordmark.

## Decision

**"Group games by" lives on the Leagues tab, not in Settings.** Each league's heading on the Leagues tab — the single-table card and the accordion header alike — carries a `SlateGroupingChip`, a menu of that league's rungs (League, Conference, Division, as `SlateGrouping.options(for:)` allows) reading the current one. Settings' "Group games by" section (2026-10-03) is removed, not duplicated. The choice still lives in `UIStateStore.slateGroupings` and still shapes the Scores slate exactly as before.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19: "the group games by league, conference, division should actually go on the leagues page … move just that setting entirely … and put it on the leagues page exclusively?" — then "why haven't we moved the leagues setting to the leagues page yet?" Moved rather than copied because a per-league choice belongs beside the league it is about, and two homes for one setting are two places that can disagree about what it says; Settings keeps the app-wide switches. The cost is discoverability from Scores, where the effect shows; the Leagues tab is one tap away and is where a reader asking "what are the NFL's groups" already goes.
