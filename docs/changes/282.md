## Decision

**The News tab has four pages: For you · Latest · Transfers · Leagues.** The
four league tabs (NCAAF, NFL, NBA, NHL) fold into one Leagues page that lists
every league in the Leagues tab's order (`League.displayOrder`: NBA, NCAAF,
NFL, NHL), each as a heading with "See more" and its five newest stories as
full-width stacked cards — photo on top, headline under it — never the
thumbnail row. Latest, the full-width list that closed For you, is its own
page and leaves For you, which now ends at its last team section.
`NewsFeedStore` fetches the four league feeds once and cuts Latest, Transfers,
Trending and the Leagues page from that one list.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19: "combine the cards on each News tab for each League into a
single League tab… listed in the order that they have them arranged in the
leagues nav item"; the cards "vertically stacked (image on top, text on
bottom), not a horizontal card". Five tabs were four of the same page with a
different filter; one page shows the whole slate in a scroll, and the stacked
card is the one the photos were brought in for. Latest moved rather than
duplicated so For you is the personal page and nothing else (Andy's pick
between the two in planning). Supersedes `docs/decisions.md` row 369's "a
league page is one section" and the five-tab row of row 358.

## Web parity

shipped — `web/src/app/(main)/news/news-view.tsx` has the same four pages; `getLeaguePages` fetches the four feeds once and `StoryCardStack` is the stacked card. The league heading prints the short name ("NCAAF"), as iOS does.

## Decision

**A Transfers page, by reading the headline.** `NewsStory.isTransaction` marks
a story as a roster move when its headline or dek carries a whole-word trade,
signing, waiver, release, extension, free-agency, portal or commitment term,
or when an ESPN topic names one outright (`trade deadline`, `transfer portal`,
`transactions`). Recaps and previews never qualify. The page is the merged
all-leagues list filtered by it, uncapped.

## Why

Andy asked for "a filtered list of just transfer related news articles."
Probed 2026-10-19: ESPN's `topic` categories cover almost none of them —
across 50-story NFL, NBA and NHL feeds the only transaction tag was one
`nba free agency`, which sat on a stat column, while "Knicks waive Bruce
Brown" and "Still awaiting trade, Connor Hellebuyck…" carried no topic at all.
A keyword reading is the only signal there is, so it is written as a pure,
tested function with its own list of words, and no bare "deal", "cut" or
"sign": every game story has a deal in it and a lead gets cut every night.
`docs/news.md` N16's college half stays parked; this is prose, not FotMob's
from–to cards.

## Web parity

shipped — `isTransaction` and `TRANSACTION_TERMS` in `web/src/lib/news.ts`, the term list copied verbatim from iOS; the mapper keeps `topic` categories as `topics`.

## Decision

**For you gets a team filter sheet.** A filter button on the News masthead
(For you only, in the slot Teams gives its plus) opens a medium/large sheet:
a checklist of every followed team in the Teams tab's A–Z order, a footer
saying unchecked teams keep their follow and leave this page, and a "Manage
teams" row that closes the sheet and switches to the Teams tab. Unchecked
teams drop out of For you's sections and out of its per-team fetch. Stored as
`news.hiddenTeams` on `UIStateStore`. Done and a swipe both dismiss.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19: "a settings affordance that opens a sheet to control what
teams show up on this page. Just checkboxes is fine… Sorting is based on the
order of your favorites. Link to your favorites." Follows have no order of
their own (a set, shown A–Z on the Teams tab), so the sheet keeps that order
and E26's "Follow order" P3 stays open. Supersedes `docs/news.md` N12's "no
news filter sheet"; N12's other half holds — it filters teams, not interests.
The link goes to the Teams tab rather than the Add-teams sheet because that is
where follows are managed, and the router already has the hook the Scores
follow prompt uses.

## Web parity

shipped — `ForYouTeamsSheet` behind a Choose teams button on the masthead, the hidden set in `localStorage` under `cfb-hub-news-hidden-teams` (`use-news-hidden-teams`), the Manage teams row to `/teams`.
