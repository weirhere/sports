## Decision

**A hidden game's Reveal score capsule is a full-width row under the records, and a hidden live header keeps its dot.** `GameHeader`'s concealed layout is two rows: the marks with the one word of status between them at their height, then `RevealScoreButton` stretched to the header's side margins, 8pt below. When the hidden game is live the pulsing dot sits inline before the word "Live", 4pt gap; a hidden final reads "Final" alone. The button's label never wraps.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-19, from the first installed build: "can we not have the button wrap text even if it has to go somewhere else? full width directly under the records. also include the pulsating green dot since it's live." In the middle column the capsule had a third of the header and "Reveal score" broke onto two lines on every device. A row of its own is the only place the label is guaranteed one line at the SE's width, and under the records is where the eye lands after the matchup. The dot reverses part one's call that a concealed page spends no live budget: the word says live, and the dot is how every other live surface in the app says it, so leaving it off made the hidden header the one live thing that didn't look live.
