## Decision

**The NHL's Stanley Cups go all-time.** `TrophyRegistry` gets the NHL's verified list (in `TrophyRegistry+NHL.swift`). `stanleyCups` covers the 1917–18 through 2013–14 seasons, keyed by the year each season opens, and the wire still derives 2014–15 on. That's 88 rows across 18 franchises. Cups are filed by franchise, so the Toronto Arenas' 1918 and St. Patricks' 1922 Cups belong to the Maple Leafs and the Black Hawks' belong to the Blackhawks. Seven seasons have no row because the winner has no successor team: the original Ottawa Senators' 1920, 1921, 1923 and 1927 Cups, the Montreal Maroons' 1926 and 1935, and the WCHL Victoria Cougars' 1925. Two more have none because no Cup was awarded (1918–19 and 2004–05). The list carries no `source`. The Canadiens' shelf reads Stanley Cups 23 and today's Senators' reads nothing.

## Why

This applies COR-94's span for hockey (the Cup from the NHL's first season, 1917–18) and its third call, that titles follow the franchise. Starting at the NHL's first season keeps the Canadiens' 1916 NHA Cup out, so Montreal reads 23 rather than the 24 the club counts. That's the span's own consequence, not a new call. The modern Senators are a 1992 expansion franchise with no claim on the original's Cups. That's the case COR-94 named by example. The rows were checked season by season against Wikipedia's "List of Stanley Cup champions". Every ESPN id is resolved in the tests from an abbreviation in the captured `nhl-standings` payload, so a mistyped id fails as a wrong total. The per-franchise totals were counted from the source list, not from the rows, and they sum to the row count (97 seasons, less 2 with no Cup and 7 defunct).

## Web parity

shipped — `web/src/lib/trophy-registry-nhl.ts` is generated row for row from the Swift list and registered in `trophy-registry.ts`. `trophy-registry.test.ts` checks the same span, per-franchise totals and the Blackhawks' merged shelf.
