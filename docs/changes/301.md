## Decision

**The scripted fixture serves college football and nothing else.**
`FixtureScoresClient` answers every other league's scoreboard with an empty
day that leaves the shared timeline alone, and its `conferenceGames` answers
only for college football. `LiveDetailHoldUITests`' `pushDetail` taps the
first on-screen copy of a row once its frame has held still across two reads.

## Why

Every `ScoreboardStore` asks with `[.fbs]` by default, so the fixture had
been handing its football Saturday to every league. When college basketball
arrived (#299), its conference sections began showing the fixture's games
too, and each of eight leagues advanced the timeline once a poll.
LiveDetailHold's first match for a row then sat off screen (a tap at y 1316
on an 874pt screen), and its GoesFinal case was waived at the 3.0.0 cut
(#300). Serving one league keeps one row per section and one tick per poll,
which is what the fixture's beats were written against. The settle check
exists because a frame read while a swipe was still moving the list put the
tap on a section header.
