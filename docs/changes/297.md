## Decision

**College football's FBS and FCS sections come back to the Conference view,
folded.** Grouped by conference (the default), Scores now reads Top 25, FBS,
FCS, then the conferences A–Z. FBS and FCS start as header rows with their game
count, and one tap opens every game in the division. They are the same
sections the League view shows, under their own ids
(`GameSection.allDivisionPrefix`, "all-conf-cfb-80"), so they take Following's
opt-in expansion instead of the open-by-default rule every other slate section
follows. Opening one is remembered across launches and days. The League view is
unchanged: Top 25, FBS and FCS, all open. This narrows 2026-10-03, which had
FBS and FCS show only when grouped by league.

## Why

Josh (via Andy, 2026-11-02): "I'm looking for an FBS tab. An overall look at
all of the games happening that day." Grouped by conference, the only way to
see an unranked game outside his conferences was to open them one by one. The
League view had it, but it sits in Settings and he never found it. Five options
were mocked in Figma (page "↳ All-FBS options"). The cost was measured as how
far down the ACC lands on a 66-game Saturday on an iPhone 17 Pro: 1.6 screens
today, 1.7 with FBS and FCS folded, 11 screens with them open (Josh's literal
ask), and 7.7 if FBS were a followed table. Folded keeps the conference view
where it is for everyone who browses by conference, and still gives Josh his
FBS tab in one tap. A separate id was the smallest way to get a different
default per grouping without a grouping-aware expansion store. The 2026-10-03
objection ("FBS and FCS are more like league based") is about putting them
*beside* conferences as peers. Folded above them, they act as the catch-all
the Top 25 already is.
