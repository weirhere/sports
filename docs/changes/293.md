## Decision

**League pages get a Team stats tab, FotMob's.** The tab is a set of sections (Top stats, Offense, Defense, Special teams for football only, Discipline). Each card shows one stat's top three, and its header opens the whole ranked table. A per-league registry (`League.teamStatDefinitions`) picks the stats. Each league makes one `site.web.api` `byteam` request per season. The values are ESPN's, but the ranks are recomputed among the page's own teams, and tied teams share a place. The tab is on the NFL, NBA and NHL league pages, their conference and division pages, FBS and its conferences, and the Top 25 page. On the Top 25 page only the teams in the poll and week it shows are ranked, under the same chips as Standings. FCS pages get no Team stats tab. The leader's number sits in an ink capsule, so no colour is added.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-25, from FotMob's league pages. Standings answer "who's in first", and this answers "who's best at what". A page ranks only its own teams so the SEC page's leaders are the SEC's. FCS pages don't get the tab because `byteam` ignores `groups=` and only answers for FBS, so an FCS page would show FBS numbers.

## Decision

**And a Player stats tab, to the left of Team stats.** Each sport is grouped in its own terms: football by phase, the NBA by shooting and all-around play, the NHL by scoring and goaltending. Each card ranks within one `byathlete` pool, sorted by its main stat, so ESPN's qualification rules hold. Pools are fetched in parallel, cached per season and ranked among the page's teams. College football requests carry `category=`, and NHL goalie tables have a 100-shot minimum. League and Top 25 pages go Standings, Games, News, Player stats, Team stats. On team pages, Standings now comes before Stats.

## Why

It closes BACKLOG's "League-wide leaders" row. Sorting each card by its own pool keeps ESPN's qualification rules, so a backup with two attempts can't lead in completion percentage. Without `category=`, college football returns every value as null.

## Decision

**The Leagues page's "Group games by" chips move into a settings sheet behind a gear.** The gear is a flat card-white circle with no glass, like the Scores gear. The search field's shadow only shows once the list scrolls, and the cards line up with the field's edges.

## Why

The chips repeated on every league card and only ever get set once. On the page's gray background, the glass gear's shadow read as a dark halo, and glass has no shadow switch.

## Web parity

n/a — the web app's grouping lives in its slate control row, and the web app has no Leagues hub page with per-league cards.

## Decision

**The StatSide ranking stays out of the app for now and keeps developing in Notion.** `scripts/statside-ranking.py` ranks teams two-thirds on résumé and one-third on performance, with opponent strength measured by a blend of scoring margin and performance. It writes the ranking to the College Football Teams 2026 database, and `docs/statside-ranking.md` holds the method and the 2024–25 backtests. This branch built the in-app version and then removed it. Commits `df554d0` and `dcf56a3` are where to restore it from.

## Why

Andy, 2026-10-28: the ranking is still changing week to week, and the app takes it back once it settles. The ranking needs about 800 game summaries a season, so if it comes back it will be as a published file, not computed on the phone.

n/a — the web app has no StatSide rating.
