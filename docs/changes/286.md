## Decision

**Live and Tight reset on launch; the funnel filter and Hide all keep persisting** (supersedes the 2026-08-29 row that persisted `liveOnly` and `scoreFilter` together, for Live and Tight only). `UIStateStore` no longer writes `ui.liveOnly` or `ui.tightOnly`; `init` clears what an older build saved, and in DEBUG alone reads the same keys from the argument domain so a fixture suite can still launch on a live slate (`-ui.liveOnly YES`). The exclusivity rule, the today-only narrowing (`liveOnly(on:)`), the permanent chip and the jump-to-today on Live-on are unchanged. The web's `use-ui-state.ts` makes the same split: `toStored` is what reaches localStorage, and `sanitize` ignores a saved Live or Tight.

## Why

Josh Vertucci, 2026-10-22, via Andy: *"when I opened up the app just now it still had my 'live games' option selected… my initial expectation was for the default to be the 'all games' option when I opened the app (similar to FotMob)."* Andy: "Live should probably reset on launch though." The 08-29 argument for persisting everything was that the labeled chip and the explanatory empty state made a saved filter legible, and that holds. What it missed is that the two filters are different kinds of thing: "SEC" is how you want the slate every day, a preference; "Live" is a question about right now, and a Live left on after Saturday's last whistle answered Tuesday with "No live games right now". Tight goes with Live because it is defined as a narrower Live and the two are exclusive. The cost to someone who did want Live already on is one tap on a chip that never moves.
