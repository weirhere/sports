## Decision

**A poll ends the way a printed poll does: Receiving votes, then Dropped out.** Under the Top 25 card on a poll's Standings tab, two monochrome cards (`PollMentionsCard`, `PollMentionRow`) list `Poll.others` most points first and `Poll.droppedOut` by the rank each team held. The rows keep the Top 25's empty rank gutter so logos and names line up, and put their one number under MOV's column, captioned PTS or PREV. They tap through to the team page and carry Follow, like a ranked row. A poll that ships neither list (CFP, a past week from the core API) draws no cards. `PollMention.record` is gone.

## Why

Both lists were already in the `/rankings` response the page fetches, decoded since 2026-10-04 and never shown. No new request, no new color: a drop-out's old rank is history, not a move, so it doesn't spend the rankings red. The record went because ESPN ships `recordSummary: "0-0"` on every mention, both live (Week 10, 2026-11-04: Northwestern at 79 points, "0-0") and in the held Final Rankings fixture, so it's a placeholder, and printing it would be wrong on every row. The caption half of COR-58 (`occurrenceLabel` over `PollWeek.label`) shipped in #293.
