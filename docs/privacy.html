StatSide Privacy Policy

Effective July 21, 2026

StatSide is a college football scores app. The short version: StatSide collects no personal data. None.

What we collect

Nothing. StatSide has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no server of its own. We never see who you are or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

The teams you follow and your interface preferences (like which sections you keep expanded) are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else, and deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

To show scores, rankings, and schedules, StatSide requests publicly available sports data over HTTPS from third-party sports data services. Like any internet request, these include your IP address as a technical necessity; StatSide adds no identifying information, and we do not operate or have access to those services' logs. No score request is tied to an identity, because the app has none to send.

Children

StatSide collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
